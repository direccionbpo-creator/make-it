Prepare an Image for Laser Engraving

Upload a photo or logo and choose Black & White, Grayscale or Dithered mode to prepare it for laser engraving. Adjust contrast, brightness, threshold and invert, preview the result, then download a high-resolution PNG (or SVG when the image is vectorizable) ready for your engraving software.

Tips for better results

  • Dither mode usually gives photos the most natural-looking engraved shading.
  • Black & White mode is best for logos and line art that need crisp, solid engraving.
  • Raise contrast slightly before engraving photos to keep detail from washing out.

Frequently asked questions

Which mode should I use for a photo?

Dither mode generally reproduces photographic shading best on an engraver, simulating grayscale tones with a fine dot pattern.

Which mode should I use for a logo?

Black & White mode gives the crisp, solid edges most logos need.

Can I get a vector file instead of PNG?

Yes, in Black & White mode an SVG download is also available, generated from the same threshold.

Does this generate G-code or laser settings?

No. This tool prepares the image only; power, speed and passes are configured in your engraving software for your specific machine and material.
