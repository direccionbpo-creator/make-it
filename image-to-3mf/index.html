Tips for better results

  • Pick base/design colors that are easy to tell apart when checking the preview.
  • Debossed models are exported as a single colored part since the design is carved into the base rather than added on top.
  • If your slicer does not support 3MF colors, use the STL download instead — the geometry is identical.

3MF vs STL

3MF

Stores geometry plus per-object color/material — the base and design stay separate parts a compatible slicer can color independently.

STL

Stores geometry only. Simpler and universally supported, but any color information is lost.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 3MF file used for?

3MF is used for 3D printing. Unlike STL, it can store multiple objects with their own materials or colors inside a single file.

Do all slicers support 3MF colors?

Most modern slicers (e.g. PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer) support 3MF and multi-object color, but always check your specific slicer and printer before relying on it.

Can I still download an STL from this tool?

Yes — every 3D print here offers both STL and 3MF downloads from the same model.

Why does debossed only produce one color?

A debossed design is a depression carved into the base, not a separate raised part, so it is exported as a single continuous colored object.
