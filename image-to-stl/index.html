Tips for better results

  • High-contrast images work best — a clear dark shape on a light background.
  • Simple logos produce cleaner geometry than detailed photographs.
  • Use "Remove small details" to clean up noisy scans or low-resolution images.
  • For deep relief, increase "Design height" — but keep it below the base thickness for debossed models.

STL vs 3MF

STL

A simple, universally supported format that stores only triangle geometry — no color, material or unit metadata. Best when your slicer or printer only accepts STL.

3MF

A modern, ZIP-based container format that can store multiple objects with separate colors and materials alongside the geometry. Use it when your slicer supports 3MF and you want to keep the base/design color split.

Frequently asked questions

Can you convert a PNG to STL?

Yes. Upload a PNG and this tool builds STL geometry from it directly in your browser — no conversion server involved.

Can a JPG be converted to STL?

Yes, JPG works the same way as PNG. Photos with clear, high-contrast subjects convert most cleanly.

What images work best for 3D printing?

Simple, high-contrast logos and line art produce the cleanest STL geometry. Busy photographs with soft edges need more threshold and detail adjustment.

Is SVG better than PNG for creating an STL?

SVG gives sharper edges after vectorization since it has no compression artifacts, but a clean, high-resolution PNG or JPG works well too.

Can I choose the thickness of the STL?

Yes — set the base thickness and design height independently in millimeters, and the 3D preview updates live.

Does the STL preserve colors?

No. STL only stores geometry, not color or material data. If you need to keep the base and design colors, download the 3MF file instead.
