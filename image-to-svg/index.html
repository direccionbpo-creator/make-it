Tips for better results

  • High-contrast logos with clean edges vectorize best.
  • Raise "Remove small details" to clean up scan noise or JPEG artifacts.
  • Increase "Detail level" for sharper corners, or lower it for smoother, simplified paths.

PNG vs SVG for manufacturing

PNG (raster)

A grid of pixels. Good for photos and engraving, but has no inherent physical scale and cannot be cut as clean vector paths without vectorizing first.

SVG (vector)

A set of mathematical paths that scale to any size without quality loss — the format laser cutters, vinyl plotters and CNC software expect for cut lines.

Frequently asked questions

Can you convert a PNG to SVG?

Yes. Upload a PNG and the tool traces its shape into vector paths automatically.

Does this keep holes in the shape, like the inside of a letter O?

Yes — contours are traced with proper hole detection and exported using the evenodd fill rule.

Can I convert a photo to SVG?

Photos can be vectorized, but results are cleanest with high-contrast subjects; busy photographic detail tends to produce noisy paths.

Is the SVG scaled to a real size?

Yes — set width and height in millimeters and the exported SVG viewBox matches that physical size.
