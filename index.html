Why it's built this way

Your files stay on your device

Every tool on this page — background removal, vectorization, STL/3MF/SVG/DXF generation — runs locally in your browser using canvas and WebGL. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Real files, not previews

Downloads are generated from the exact geometry shown in the preview: valid binary STL, zipped 3MF with per-part color, DXF (AC1015/LWPOLYLINE), SVG and printable PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Every tool works immediately after you upload an image — there's no sign-up and no installation.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

For every tool on this site, image processing (background removal, vectorization, 3D geometry, file export) runs locally in your browser. Your file is never sent anywhere.

What image formats can I upload?

PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP and SVG. High-contrast logos and line art produce the cleanest results across every tool.

Which file format should I download?

For 3D printing, choose 3MF if your slicer supports it (it keeps base/design colors); choose STL for maximum compatibility. For laser cutting, CNC and vinyl, SVG and DXF both carry the same vector paths — pick whichever your software imports.

Is this really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use, with no limit tied to an account since none is required.
