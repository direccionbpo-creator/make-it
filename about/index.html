About Make It

Make It is a collection of free browser-based tools for turning images and designs into files for digital fabrication.

Most people who want to 3D print, laser cut or stencil a logo don't own — or want to learn — CAD software. They have an image and a machine, and just need a file that connects the two. That's the entire idea behind Make It: upload an image, choose what you want to make, download a real file.

Every tool on this site — background removal, vectorization, contour tracing, 3D mesh generation, STL/3MF/SVG/DXF/PDF export — runs locally in your browser. Nothing you upload is sent to a server, stored, or seen by anyone.

Make It is free to use and supported by advertising, clearly marked as such wherever it appears. There is no account system, no upload limit tied to a plan, and no feature hidden behind a paywall.

What we don't do

We don't generate G-code — machine-specific toolpaths depend on your printer, laser, router, tool and material, and are configured in your own slicer or CAM software. We don't claim a format does more than it does: STL doesn't store color, and a DXF or SVG needs to be imported into your machine's software before it can cut anything.
