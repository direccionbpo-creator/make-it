Make It / Privacy

Privacy

Your images are processed locally in your browser and are never uploaded to our servers, for every tool on this site.

Your files

When you upload an image to any tool on Make It — Image to STL, 3MF, SVG, DXF, laser cut, laser engrave, CNC, vinyl or stencil — it is decoded directly into your browser's memory (an HTML canvas). All processing — background removal, thresholding, vectorization, 3D mesh generation, and file export — runs as JavaScript in your browser. The image and the files you generate from it are never transmitted to our servers, stored by us, or visible to us in any way.

Closing the tab or navigating away discards everything; nothing persists unless you download it yourself.

What we do collect

We use standard, privacy-respecting analytics to understand which tools are used (for example, that an "upload" or "download" event happened), and we may show advertising. Neither the analytics nor the advertising on this site has access to the contents of your image — they only see that an action occurred, not what you uploaded.

If that ever changes

If we add a tool in the future that genuinely requires server-side processing, that tool's own page and this page will say so explicitly before you use it — we won't rely on you assuming it works like the rest of the site.

Cookies and advertising

Pages on this site reserve space for advertising, clearly marked "ADVERTISEMENT." Ad networks may use cookies to serve relevant ads; you can control this through your browser settings.
