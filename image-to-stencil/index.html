Convert Image to a Stencil

Upload a photo, logo or lettering and this tool turns it into a physically cuttable stencil: it detects "island" shapes that would otherwise fall out — like the inside of an O, A, B, P or R — and automatically adds small bridges to keep them attached. Set the bridge width and count, preview the result, and download SVG or a printable PDF.

Tips for better results

  • Bold, simple letters and logos make the sturdiest stencils.
  • Increase bridge width for stencil material that flexes a lot, like thin plastic sheet.
  • More bridges per island add strength but leave more small gaps in the final painted result.

Frequently asked questions

How does it keep the middle of an O from falling out?

It detects enclosed "hole" contours and cuts small evenly-spaced gaps — bridges — into both the hole and its surrounding shape, so the cut material stays physically connected.

Can I control how many bridges are added?

Yes — set the bridge count per island and the bridge width in millimeters.

Can I print the stencil directly?

Yes — the PDF download is sized to the exact physical dimensions you set, ready to print at 100% scale.

What material can I cut the stencil from?

This tool prepares the cut file only; paper, acetate, vinyl and thin plastic are all common stencil materials depending on your cutting method.
